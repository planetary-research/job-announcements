How do I create a job announcement?

To create a job announcment, click on the link to sign in and then authenticate with your ORCID account. Next, click the link Create in the options at the top of the screen. This will bring you to a new page, and you will be asked to provide some information about the job announcement in a form and to provide a job description using a text editor. If the job description was already written, it should only take about three minutes to publish your announcement.

All job announcments are set to expire one day after the application deadline. If no deadline was provided, the announcment will expire after one year. The jobs database will be periodically purged of expired announcments.

How do I modify a job announcement?

To modify a job announcment, it is only necessary to sign in and then click on the Edit/Delete link in the options at the top of the page. This will bring you to a page that will list of all the annuoncmenents that you created. By clicking on one of your announcements, you will be redirected to a page where you can modify all the fields that you entered previously. You will also have the option to close the announcement, which will keep the announcement in the database but not show it on the home page. If the announcement was closed, you can choose to publish it, which will make it visible on the home page. You will also have the option to delete the annoucement entirely from the database.

How long are job announcements shown?

Job announcements will be shown on the website up until one day after the application deadline. When four months have passed since the deadline, the job announcement will be automatically closed: This means that the job will remain in the database, but only the job owner will have access to the announcement. When one year has passed since the deadline, the announcement will be deleted from the database.

If the job announcement doesn't have an application deadline, it will be deleted automatically after one year has passed since it was posted. If the job doesn't have an application deadline and it was since closed, it will be deleted one year after the announcement was created.

Are any data stored with ORCID?

No. Authenticating with ORCID is used only to prove that you are the owner of an ORCID iD. This allow users to create a new job announcement or to manage their previously created announcements. No data are stored on ORCID servers.

Can I see the source code?

Yes! The code that we use is available on Github and everyone is free to inspect it. We encourage users to inform us of any bugs, and we are open to contributions that will improve the functionality of the application.
